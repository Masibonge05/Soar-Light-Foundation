About Us

Committed to uplifting children from underprivileged communities

Who We Are

Soar Light Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation committed to uplifting children and young people from underprivileged communities. We exist to restore dignity, inspire hope, and create opportunities for learners to thrive academically, socially, and personally.

Through education support, sanitary pad distribution, food and clothing drives, and community outreach, we address both immediate needs and long-term development. Our work is driven by compassion, volunteerism, and a belief that every child deserves the opportunity to soar.

Our Story

Soar Light Foundation was founded by individuals who recognised the deep challenges faced by children in underprivileged communities—from lack of basic necessities to limited access to education and guidance. What began as a simple desire to help has grown into a structured organisation committed to lighting the path for children to rise, hope, and dream beyond their current circumstances.

Our Vision

To see orphaned and vulnerable children supported, protected, and empowered to live with dignity and hope.

What We Do

Our programmes address both immediate needs and long-term development

Sanitary Pads & Dignity Drive

We distribute sanitary pads to school-going girls to promote menstrual health, dignity, and consistent school attendance.

Education Support & Career Guidance

We assist learners with university and college applications, bursary and funding applications, and provide career guidance and mentorship.

Clothing & School Uniform Drive

We collect and distribute clothing and school uniforms to learners from under-resourced households.

Food Support & Nutrition

We provide food parcels and run soup kitchens to support vulnerable children and families during times of need.

Volunteerism & Community Outreach

We mobilise volunteers and partners to support our initiatives and strengthen community involvement.

Child Welfare & Protection

We promote and protect the rights and dignity of all children in our care and programmes.

Our Beneficiaries

Our programmes primarily support:

Primary and secondary school learners
Children from disadvantaged communities
Girl learners in need of dignity support
Families facing socio-economic hardship

Making a difference in the lives of children and families
